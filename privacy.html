Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The Success Studios app is an internal tool used only by Success Studios to manage its own YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

  • Through Google sign-in, with the account owner's permission: the ability to upload videos to, and read basic information and analytics for, the YouTube channels that Success Studios owns.
  • No other Google data (no email, contacts, files or calendar).

How the data is used

Only to publish our own videos and to measure how they perform (views, watch time, retention), so we can make better videos.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and channel analytics are stored on Success Studios' own computer. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Success Studios' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted on request.
